Brand Guide

Learning Morocco Tours is the educational and group-travel brand of Explore Morocco DMC. The identity combines a Moroccan horseshoe arch, an 8-point star (khatam), the sun over the dunes and an open book: travel that teaches.

Logo

Primary logo, for light backgrounds

Reversed logo, for navy or photo backgrounds

Emblem: stamps, watermark, merchandise

Square icon: favicon, social profile picture, WhatsApp

Do
  • Keep clear space equal to the arch width around the logo
  • Use the reversed logo on dark photos and navy
  • Minimum width: 140px for the full logo, 24px for the icon
Don't
  • Stretch, rotate or add shadows or glow effects
  • Change the colours or place it on busy, low-contrast images
  • Re-type the wordmark in another font
