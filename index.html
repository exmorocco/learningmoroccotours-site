01 · Explore Morocco

Morocco is the classroom. We make it unforgettable.

Learning Morocco Tours is the student-travel brand of Explore Morocco DMC, based in Tangier. We work only with students and their teachers, turning Morocco's cities, companies, culture and landscapes into a real classroom.

  • Study trips for secondary schools, universities and vocational training centres (CFA)
  • Company and university visits, workshops and meetings with professionals
  • Cultural and language immersion: medinas, artisans, Arabic & Darija
  • Supervised desert and coast activities that bring the class together

FAQ

Questions from teachers

Is Morocco safe for student groups?

Yes. Our local team plans every step with vetted accommodation, licensed guides and private transport, and a coordinator stays with your group and is reachable 24/7.

Which students do you work with?

Secondary schools, universities, vocational training centres (CFA) and youth organisations. Each programme is adapted to the age, level and objectives of your students.

Can you help with Erasmus+ or other funding applications?

Yes. We provide the detailed programme, quotes and documents you need for your mobility or funding application.

What is included in a student trip?

Accommodation, meals, private transport, visits, workshops, guides, activities and on-site coordination. Flights can be arranged on request.

How far in advance should we book?

Ideally 2 to 4 months before departure, to secure accommodation, visits and the best prices for your group.
